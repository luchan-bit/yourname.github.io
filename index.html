1 閱讀與範文分析 (18分)

Q1. 根據《魚我所欲也》，以下哪一項最能反映孟子「捨生取義」的思想？ 2分

答案：B。
理據：孟子指出人心中有比生命更珍貴的東西（義），也有比死亡更厭惡的東西（不義），因此即使面對生死抉擇，亦能堅守道義。

Q2. 在《論君子》中，孔子說「君子坦蕩蕩，小人長戚戚」。試解釋「坦蕩蕩」在文中的含義，並說明其與君子修養的關係。 4分

參考答案：
「坦蕩蕩」意指心胸平坦寬廣。君子行為合乎正道，問心無愧，因此心情平靜舒暢；相反，小人心中充滿私慾與算計，經常憂心忡忡（長戚戚）。這反映了君子內在修養帶來的精神境界。

Q3. 短文寫作：試以一個日常生活例子，說明如何體現「克己復禮」的精神。 12分

寫作指引： 1. 先定義「克己復禮」（約束私慾，回歸禮法）；2. 舉出具體場景（如圖書館安靜、排隊禮讓）；3. 總結其對個人品格提升的意義。

3 Section A (15 Marks)

Q1. If $k + (3-k)i = 5 - 2i$, where $k$ is a real number, find the value of $k$. 3M

Solution:
By comparing the real parts: $k = 5$.
Check imaginary part: $3 - k = 3 - 5 = -2$, which matches.
Final Answer: $k = 5$.

Q2. Given the quadratic equation $x^2 - 6x + (m-1) = 0$. If the equation has no real roots, find the range of values of $m$. 4M

Solution:
For no real roots, $\Delta < 0$.
$\Delta = b^2 - 4ac = (-6)^2 - 4(1)(m-1) < 0$
$36 - 4m + 4 < 0$
$40 < 4m$
Answer: $m > 10$.

Q3. Long Question: Let $f(x) = x^2 - 4x + 7$.
(a) Express $f(x)$ in vertex form.
(b) Describe the transformation from $y = x^2$ to $y = f(x)$. 8M

Solution:
(a) $f(x) = (x^2 - 4x + 4) - 4 + 7 = (x-2)^2 + 3$.
(b) Shift the graph of $y = x^2$ to the right by 2 units and then up by 3 units.

4 MCQ & Structured Questions (15 Marks)

Q1. Which of the following ions has the same electronic arrangement as a Neon atom? 2M

Ans: B.
Neon is (2, 8). Magnesium atom is (2, 8, 2); $Mg^{2+}$ loses 2 electrons to become (2, 8).

Q2. Displacement Reaction: When a piece of Zinc is added to Copper(II) Sulphate solution, what observations would you expect? Explain with an ionic equation. 5M

Answer:
Observations: The blue solution fades (becomes colorless), and a reddish-brown solid (copper) is deposited on the zinc surface.
Ionic Equation: $Zn(s) + Cu^{2+}(aq) \rightarrow Zn^{2+}(aq) + Cu(s)$.
Reason: Zinc is more reactive than copper and displaces it from the solution.

Q3. Corrosion: State two factors necessary for iron to rust, and explain how 'galvanization' prevents rusting even if the zinc coating is scratched. 8M

Key Points: 1. Factors: Water and Oxygen. 2. Galvanization: Zinc is more reactive than iron. It acts as a sacrificial metal, losing electrons and corroding preferentially to protect the iron underneath.

5 Tectonics & Map Skills (18 Marks)

Q1. At which type of plate boundary is new oceanic crust formed? 2M

Ans: B. Divergent boundary.
Magma rises at mid-ocean ridges to form new crust.

Q2. Discuss why the San Francisco area is prone to frequent earthquakes. Refer to the type of plate boundary involved. 6M

Answer:
San Francisco lies along the San Andreas Fault, which is a transform plate boundary. The Pacific Plate and the North American Plate slide past each other horizontally. Friction causes the plates to lock, and when the accumulated stress is suddenly released, a shallow-focus earthquake occurs.

Q3. Map Calculation: If the vertical distance between two points is 400m and the horizontal distance on a 1:20,000 map is 5cm, calculate the actual gradient. 10M

Solution:
1. Actual Horizontal Distance = $5 \text{ cm} \times 20,000 = 100,000 \text{ cm} = 1,000 \text{ m}$.
2. Gradient = $\frac{\text{Vertical Distance}}{\text{Horizontal Distance}} = \frac{400}{1000} = \mathbf{0.4}$ (or 1:2.5).

6 資料回應與基礎知識 (18分)

Q1. 根據《基本法》，香港特別行政區擁有高度自治權，但下列哪項權力歸屬於中央人民政府？ 2分

答案：B。
理據：根據《基本法》第十三條及第十四條，中央人民政府負責管理與香港特別行政區有關的外交事務及防務。

Q2. 試簡述《憲法》與《基本法》的關係。 6分

參考要點：
1. 《憲法》是國家的根本大法，具有最高法律地位；2. 《基本法》根據《憲法》制定，其法律效力來源於《憲法》；3. 《基本法》是落實「一國兩制」方針的法律保障，規定了香港特區實行的制度。

Q3. 資料分析：有人認為「國民身份認同」與「國際視野」可以並存，你是否同意？請舉例說明。 10分

評分指引： 1. 表明立場；2. 解釋兩者不矛盾的原因（例如：認識國家歷史可與學習全球議題互補）；3. 舉例（如：參與國際志願服務、推廣中國文化到世界）。
