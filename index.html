Q1. 根據《論仁、論孝、論君子》，孔子認為「仁者」面對貧困時會有什麼表現？ 2M

A. 怨天尤人，設法擺脫

B. 即使貧困，亦不離棄仁德

C. 視若無睹，追求名利

D. 感到羞恥，隱居避世

答案：B。 理據：孔子云「仁者安仁」，強調仁德是君子在任何處境（包括貧困）都應堅持的原則。

Q2. 試解釋「克己復禮為仁」中「克己」的含義。 4M

答案： 「克己」意指約束、克制自己的慾望。孔子強調要透過克制主觀私慾，使言行舉止回歸到「禮」的規範，這就是實踐仁德的方法。

Time: 1.25 Hours | Total: 45 Marks

Q1. If the quadratic equation $x^2 - (k+1)x + 9 = 0$ has equal real roots, find the value(s) of $k$. 4M

Solution: For equal real roots, $\Delta = 0$.
$b^2 - 4ac = [-(k+1)]^2 - 4(1)(9) = 0$
$(k+1)^2 - 36 = 0 \Rightarrow (k+1)^2 = 36$
$k+1 = 6$ or $k+1 = -6$.
$k = 5$ or $k = -7$.

Chemistry

Syllabus:

  • Planet Earth (Microscopic World I): Bonding, Atomic Structure.
  • Metals: Reactivity, Extraction, Corrosion.

Q1. Explain why graphite can conduct electricity but diamond cannot, even though both are made of carbon. 4M

Answer: 1. In graphite, each carbon atom is covalently bonded to only 3 others, leaving one delocalized electron per atom that can carry charge. 2. In diamond, each carbon atom is bonded to 4 others in a rigid tetrahedral structure; all valence electrons are involved in bonding, so no free electrons exist.

Geography

Syllabus:

  • Opportunities and Risks: Plate Tectonics, Earthquakes, Volcanoes.
  • Map Skills: Grid references, gradients, cross-sections.

Q1. Discuss how plate boundaries contribute to the formation of tsunamis. 6M

Answer: Tsunamis are primarily formed at convergent (destructive) plate boundaries. When an oceanic plate subducts under a continental plate, stress builds up. A sudden release of energy (earthquake) causes vertical displacement of the seabed, which displaces a massive volume of water, creating powerful waves.

Q1. 下列哪一項正確描述了《基本法》與《憲法》的關係？ 2M

A. 《基本法》具有高於《憲法》的法律效力

B. 《憲法》是《基本法》的立法依據與效力來源

C. 《基本法》可以修改《憲法》的條文

D. 兩者在香港的應用範圍完全相同

答案：B。 理據：《憲法》是國家的根本大法，具備最高的法律地位。根據《憲法》第 31 條，國家才有權設立特別行政區並透過全國人大制定《基本法》。
