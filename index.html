Q3. 短問答：孔子如何解釋「克己復禮為仁」？ [4]
參考答案： 意指約束自己的慾望，使言行舉止符合禮的規範。只要能做到這一點，天下的人都會歸許其仁德。具體實踐包括：非禮勿視、非禮勿聽、非禮勿言、非禮勿動。
Q4. 長題目：試說明在記敘文中，「藉事抒情」相比「直接抒情」有何優點？ [8]
參考答案： 1. 感染力更強：透過具體細節描寫，讓讀者感同身受，而非枯燥地說教。2. 情感含蓄真摯：情感寄託在事物或行為中，更加深刻。3. 避免空洞：有事實支撐的情感更具說服力。

2. Mathematics - 16分

Q5. For the quadratic equation $x^2 + 4x + 4 = 0$, what is the number of real roots? [2]
Q6. Given $f(x) = x^2 - 4x + 7$. What is the coordinates of the vertex? [2]
Q7. Short Question: Solve the equation $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$. [4]
Sample Answer: Factoring: $(x-2)(x-3) = 0$. Therefore, $x = 2$ or $x = 3$.
Q8. Long Question: The product of two consecutive positive integers is 156. Find the integers by setting up a quadratic equation. [8]
Sample Answer: Let $x$ and $x+1$ be the integers. $x(x+1) = 156 \Rightarrow x^2 + x - 156 = 0$. Using quadratic formula or factoring: $(x+13)(x-12) = 0$. Since they are positive, $x = 12$. The integers are 12 and 13.

3. Chemistry - 16分

Q9. Which of the following elements has the electronic arrangement 2, 8, 2? [2]
Q10. Why do ionic compounds have high melting points? [2]
Q11. Short Question: Draw the electron diagram (dot-and-cross) for Sodium Chloride. [4]
Describe the transfer of electrons in text if drawing is not possible.
Sample Answer: Na loses 1 electron to become $Na^+ [2,8]$. Cl gains 1 electron to become $Cl^- [2,8,8]$. Shown as $[Na]^+$ and $[Cl \text{ with 8 dots}]^-$.
Q12. Long Question: Explain why Magnesium is more reactive than Copper based on their reactions with dilute acids. [8]
Sample Answer: Mg reacts vigorously with dilute HCl to produce Hydrogen gas (bubbles). Cu does not react with dilute HCl. This shows Mg has a higher tendency to lose electrons and form ions than Cu.

Q18. On a map with a scale of 1:50,000, what does 2cm represent in reality? [2]
Q19. Short Question: Briefly explain how a "Tsunami" is formed. [4]
Sample Answer: It is usually caused by an underwater earthquake at a destructive plate boundary. The sudden vertical displacement of the seabed displaces a huge volume of water, creating powerful waves.
Q20. Long Question: Evaluate the effectiveness of "Earthquake-proof buildings" in LEDCs (Less Economically Developed Countries). [8]
Sample Answer: While effective, these technologies are expensive. In LEDCs, high costs and lack of skilled labor make wide adoption difficult. Governments often prioritize basic needs over retrofitting, leading to higher casualties during seismic events compared to MEDCs.

6. 公民與社會發展科 (CSD) - 20分

Q21. 根據《基本法》，香港特別行政區的法律制度基礎是什麼？ [2]
Q22. 下列哪一項體現了國家對香港行使「全面管治權」？ [2]
Q23. 短問答：請簡述「一國兩制」中「一國」與「兩制」的關係。 [6]
參考答案： 「一國」是「兩制」的前提和基礎。「兩制」從屬並派生於「一國」，並在「一國」之內共存。沒有「一國」，就沒有「兩制」。
Q24. 長題目：試探討《基本法》如何保障香港居民的權利與自由？請舉出兩個例子說明。 [10]
參考答案： 1. 法律保障：基本法第三章詳細列明居民享有言論、新聞、出版、集會、結社等自由。 2. 司法保障：香港擁有獨立的司法權和終審權，確保居民受到公平審訊。例子：居民可對行政機關的行為提起訴訟（司法覆核）。
